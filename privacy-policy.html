Privacy information

Last updated: 27 October 2026 Applies to: this demonstration website

1. What this website is

Ivory & Sage Dental Studio is a fictional brand created by Uvron for a portfolio demonstration. This website does not represent a real clinic, dental practice or healthcare provider. The named team, testimonials, address, phone number, email address and opening hours are all sample content.

Because the clinic is fictional, the privacy considerations are deliberately limited. This page exists to show how a privacy notice could be presented, not to describe a real data-processing operation.

2. The sample appointment form

The form on the contact page asks for a name, email address, optional phone number, treatment interest, preferred date and time, an optional message and a consent checkbox.

  • Validation happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript.
  • No network request is made when you submit the form.
  • The information is not written to a database, file, cookie or browser storage.
  • No real clinic, inbox or third party receives the information.
  • Submitting the form only clears the fields and displays a demonstration confirmation.

Please do not enter real medical, financial or personal information into the demo form. If you have already typed something sensitive, reload the page to clear the form.

3. Third-party resources

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and animation libraries from jsDelivr. Those services may receive standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the page making the request, under their own policies. This demo does not use analytics, advertising pixels or tracking scripts.

If those external resources are unavailable, the website still works. Text falls back to system fonts and motion is treated as optional.

4. Cookies and local storage

This demonstration does not set cookies, use local storage or use session storage. It does not remember form entries between visits. The current year shown in the footer is calculated in your browser and is not stored.

5. Your choices

  • You can browse the entire site without submitting the form.
  • You can reload the page at any time to clear any values typed into the form.
  • You can block third-party requests in your browser; the layout and content remain usable.
  • You can ask questions about the demo through the sample email below, understanding that it is a placeholder address.
