Dr. Ananya Rao

Preventive and restorative dentistry

Ananya is the fictional founder of Ivory & Sage. Her imagined approach is unhurried: understand the person, explain what is visible, and agree on a plan that makes sense outside the treatment room.

How we would work

One team, one conversation.

A small studio only works if the experience holds together between people. These are the sample habits the team is built around.

Handovers that sound human

Notes are written to be understood by the next person, not just filed. No one should have to repeat their story from scratch.

Time protected at the chair

The sample schedule leaves room for questions, pauses and the appointment that needs a little longer.

Consent, every time

What happens next is agreed, not assumed. That includes the choice to pause or come back another day.
