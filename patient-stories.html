More sample notes

Different reasons. Different starting points.

These are not before-and-after claims. They are short, fictional notes about what clear communication could feel like.

All quotes fictional
01

Context over spectacle

Feedback should say what kind of visit it was and what it can reasonably represent. A single story is not a promise.

02

Consent before publishing

Real testimonials require clear permission, a right to withdraw and care around identifying details. This demo uses none.

03

No pressure by proxy

Stories can help someone feel less alone. They should never be used to imply a guaranteed result or rush a decision.
