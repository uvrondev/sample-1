Care for small cavities and worn edges using a tooth-coloured restoration, with the process and aftercare explained first.

Root canal treatment

Treatment for an inflamed or infected tooth, planned around diagnosis, comfort and a clear follow-up pathway.

Restorative care

Crowns & bridges

A made-to-measure restoration to protect a tooth or replace a gap, planned from a digital scan and a discussed shade match.

Discuss a crown or bridge
06

Restorative care

Dental implants

A planning-led option for replacing a missing tooth, assessed case by case with imaging and a clear timeline.

Good to know Suitability depends on bone, gum health and general health. The demo makes no promises about outcomes.

Ask about implants
07

Restorative & urgent

Tooth extractions

When a tooth cannot be kept, a considered removal with aftercare and replacement options discussed beforehand.

Good to know Keeping a tooth is usually the first question. Removal is discussed only when it is the more reasonable option.

Book a consultation
08

Orthodontics

Braces & clear aligners

A measured approach to straighter teeth, with suitability, timelines and retainers explained before treatment starts.

Good to know Aligners are not right for every bite. The sample consultation compares options rather than pushing one.

Book an orthodontic chat
09

Cosmetic care

Professional teeth whitening

In-studio or take-home whitening with a shade review, sensitivity guidance and realistic expectations.

Good to know Results vary. The fictional studio would never promise a specific shade or a pain-free experience.

Ask about whitening
10

Cosmetic care

Veneers & smile design

A conservative, planning-led conversation about shape, shade and proportion — not a one-size-fits-all smile.

Good to know Tooth structure is precious. The demo emphasises restraint, consent and a clear understanding of trade-offs.

Book a smile consultation

Everyday & restorative

Gum care

Support for bleeding gums, gum disease and maintenance, with a plan reviewed at each visit.

Good to know Gum health is part of every examination, not a separate add-on. Home care matters as much as the visit.

Ask about gum care

Children’s dentistry

A gentle introduction to dental care for children, with visits paced around your child and parent or guardian.
