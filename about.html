The studio

A studio built around the conversation.

Ivory & Sage is a fictional dental-studio concept for Bengaluru. It imagines a practice where the first appointment is a conversation, not a sales pitch — and where the plan makes sense before anything begins.

Our point of view

Dentistry should feel less like a transaction.

The concept began with a simple observation: many people leave a dental appointment without really understanding what happened, what it cost, or what comes next. Ivory & Sage is an attempt to design that experience differently.

The imagined studio pairs a light-filled, residential-feeling setting with a preventive-first approach. Appointments are paced to leave room for questions. Treatment options are explained in plain language, including the option to wait or do nothing. And the same small team follows the details from visit to visit.

This is not a real clinic. It is a complete front-end demonstration of how a thoughtful dental brand could look, speak, and guide someone toward an appointment.

What we value

Three ideas that shape the visit.

These are the principles the fictional studio is built around — and the standard its sample team would be held to.

Listen before recommending

Your concerns, history and priorities come first. The examination follows the conversation, not the other way around.

Make the plan legible

Options, trade-offs and likely next steps are explained without pressure. You should be able to repeat the plan back in your own words.

Keep care consistent

A small team, clear records and a familiar rhythm. Prevention is treated as the main event, not an upsell.

Sample environment

A calm room, a clear plan, and time to ask the second question.

A visit, imagined

What a first appointment could feel like.

This is a sample format, not a real clinical protocol. It is designed to show how the studio would set expectations before a patient arrives.

  1. 01

    Arrive without paperwork

    A short form in advance, a quiet welcome, and no clipboard at the door.

  2. 02

    Talk through the reason for your visit

    What has been bothering you, what you have tried, and what you are hoping for.

  3. 03

    Examine and explain together

    Findings are shown on screen where possible, with time for questions before a plan is discussed.

  4. 04

    Leave with next steps in writing

    A clear summary, realistic options, and no pressure to decide on the day.

The shared standard

Four habits we would want to keep.

Principles are easy to write. The demo imagines the small, repeatable behaviours that would make them real.

01

Explain the why

Every recommendation starts with what was seen and why it matters.

02

Show the options

Including the choice to wait, when that is clinically reasonable.

03

Agree the pace

Breaks, signals and a plan that respects your comfort and budget.

04

Write it down

A simple summary after the visit, so nothing depends on memory.
